# Frame-by-frame review

Check every direction of every animation. When something reads wrong, **fix the model and re-render**.

Lessons from the video (the "what didn't work" list):

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Enemies in the heroes' color read as heroes | Enemies get a distinct base (dark metal); hero color only as trim |
| A ring around a helmet reads as a target | Avoid ring/circle shapes on the head |
| A weapon pointing at the camera disappears in the front view | Aim weapons slightly to the side |
| Design too similar to existing characters | Discard it and start over; archive it |

Also check: silhouette identifiable at sprite size, family traits present, palette and light unchanged from the series.
